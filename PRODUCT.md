# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People using AyuGram Desktop to message, manage chats, and access Telegram features with a faster, more flexible desktop workflow.

## Product Purpose

Provide a reliable Telegram desktop client with AyuGram-specific enhancements such as ghost mode, anti-recall, history tools, premium-related helpers, and quality-of-life improvements for daily messaging.

## Brand Personality

Confident, precise, privacy-minded.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboards, over-decorated modal UI, inconsistent control styling, and novelty-driven interactions that get in the way of familiar Telegram workflows.

## Design Principles

Keep the interface familiar before being expressive.
Make dense information readable with clear hierarchy and spacing.
Treat state changes, warnings, and actions as first-class UI.
Prefer calm polish over flashy decoration.
Respect the user’s flow and reduce friction in common tasks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, strong keyboard accessibility, readable text at varied DPI scales, and reduced-motion-friendly interactions.
